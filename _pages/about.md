I am a Staff Research Scientist at Google, Intrinsic, based in Mountain View, California. My research focuses on physical intelligence and 3D vision, with an emphasis on stereo matching, depth estimation, and robotic manipulation.

Previously, I was a Senior Research Scientist at Holiday Robotics and a Principal Engineer at Samsung Electronics. My work spans robust stereo depth estimation in transparent, reflective, and cluttered scenes, 6-DoF object pose estimation, and vision systems for industrial automation. Earlier research explored computational imaging for super-resolution microscopy and low-dose CT reconstruction.
